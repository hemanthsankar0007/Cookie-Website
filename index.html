about your health

HealthCheck Pro helps you understand your body mass index (BMI) and provides personalized recommendations for maintaining a healthy weight. Our easy-to-use calculator takes into account your age, height, weight, and gender to give you accurate health insights.

BMI is a useful measure of overweight and obesity. It's calculated using your weight and height to estimate if you're in a healthy weight range for your height.
